STEP 3 · STRESS-TEST · 30 MIN
Break the optimistic numbers and name the killer.
01List the five assumptions in your unit: cost, price, volume, churn, acquisition.
02Move one against you. Recalculate the margin. Then the next. Each move takes five to seven minutes.
03The first one that breaks the margin is your economics killer. Name it in one line.
HOW TO MOVE AN ASSUMPTION
For each one, ask: "what if this is twice as bad as I hope?" Churn at 50, not 10. Acquisition at €5, not €1. Price at €4, not €8. If the margin survives, the assumption is safe. If it flips, you have your killer.
