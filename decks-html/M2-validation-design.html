TWO STANDING RULES
Two rules every question must pass.
RULE ONE
Never mention your product.
If you frame the problem around your fix, you only hear about your fix.
RULE TWO
Ask what they actually did.
Past behaviour is recorded evidence, while future intent is guesswork.

FROM ASSUMPTION TO HYPOTHESIS
Turn your riskiest assumption into a hypothesis you can disprove.

WE ARE WRONG IF
Name the behaviour you'd observe that disconfirms the belief, and write it before you ask anyone.
TOMÁS'S HYPOTHESIS
We believe the 7pm case keeps Tomás from playing because the easier thing wins, even though the guitar is tuned and in reach. We'd be wrong if teens named a different breaking point, unprompted.

STAGE 3
Fix the questions in three moves.
These rules hold whether you wrote the question or a tool did.
01Strip the product. Cut every word that names your fix.
02Replace "would" with "did". Ask about what they already did.
03Open a door to no. Each question must invite a disconfirming answer.
EVERY QUESTION CAN COME BACK NO
