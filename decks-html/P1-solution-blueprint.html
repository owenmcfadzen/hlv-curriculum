WHY ROUGH WINS
Keep it rough on purpose.
Fidelity is how finished your prototype looks. Grow what it does, keep how it looks plain.
ROUGH
Boxes and words, no colour.
People tell you whether the idea works, because there is nothing pretty to be polite about.
POLISHED
Looks like a real app.
People say it looks nice and stay kind, and you learn nothing about the idea.
